The practice

Most people are never taught how property creates wealth. I was not either, until I started reading the numbers for a living.

Every comparative market analysis I send is written by hand. By the time an offer is written, the comparables are already read, the carrying cost is on the page, and you know what happens to the number if rates move. If a property is wrong for you, I will say so.
