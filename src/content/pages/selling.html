Selling your home in the Greater Toronto Area

A listing price is an argument. Mine comes with the comparables attached, so you can see what the number is standing before you agree to it.

How a sale runs

Three stages, and you always know which one you’re in.

  1. 01

    Preparation

    A written analysis of the last ninety days on your street, before anything is listed. What sold, what didn’t, and what made the difference.

  2. 02

    Market

    Photography, staging notes and the pricing band, with the reasoning attached to each one. You’ll know why the number sits where it sits.

  3. 03

    Close

    Offers read out loud, term by term, so the decision is yours and not the room’s.

The Analysis

Takes an afternoon, done by hand.

An algorithm can't see the renovation, the lot line, or the ninety days of offers that never registered. You get a range rather than one number, the evidence under it, and what would move it away.

If the read is to wait, i'll tell you, with the numbers that say so. Even though waiting doesn't pay me this year.

What I need from you

Very little, and none of it before you’ve decided anything.

  1. 01

    The address, so I can pull the street.

  2. 02

    Anything you’ve changed since you bought, with rough dates.

  3. 03

    Your timing, and how firm it is.

That’s enough for a first analysis. The rest can wait until the number makes sense to you.
