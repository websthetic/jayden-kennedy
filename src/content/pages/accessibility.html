If any part of this site, or of working with me, is difficult to use, tell me and I will either fix it or find another way to get you the same information." - "This statement covers soldbykennedy.ca. It was built to be usable with a keyboard alone, with a screen reader, at high zoom, and with motion turned off. That is the standard it is held to, not an aspiration for later." - "Ontario's Human Rights Code requires accommodation regardless of the size of a business, and the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act sets out web requirements for larger organisations. This site is built to the standard below whether or not that second one applies to a practice this size." standard: heading: "The standard" paragraphs: - "The target is the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines version 2.1, Level AA. That covers colour contrast, keyboard operability, text alternatives for images, clear focus indicators, and content that survives being resized to 200 per cent. Where the AODA is referenced elsewhere it names version 2.0; 2.1 is the later version and includes everything 2.0 asks for." - "The site is partially conformant with that standard. Partial conformance means most of the site meets it and some parts do not. The parts that do not are named below rather than left for you to discover." built: heading: "What is built in" items: - term: "Keyboard access" description: "Every link, button, form field and filter can be reached and operated with the keyboard alone. A skip link at the top of each page jumps straight past the navigation to the main content." - term: "Visible focus" description: "Whatever the keyboard is currently on is marked. Most controls carry an outline; form fields darken and thicken their rule instead, which suits a set of fields drawn as lines rather than boxes. Nothing is left unmarked." - term: "Touch targets" description: "Controls are at least 44 pixels tall, including the small filter buttons, which gain padding rather than shrinking their box." - term: "Reduced motion" description: "Section reveals, card hovers and the scroll animation on the selling page all respect the operating system's reduce-motion setting. With it on, content appears without movement." - term: "Video" description: "Clips autoplay muted, only while in view, one at a time, and always with a visible pause control. Captions are burned into every clip rather than depending on a player setting." - term: "Text and contrast" description: "Body text is set in a size and colour that meets the AA contrast ratio against every background used on the site, including the green and cocoa panels. Text resizes without breaking layout, and no meaning is carried by colour alone." - term: "Forms" description: "Every field has a label that stays attached to it, and on all but one control that label is visible on the page rather than read out only. Placeholders show the expected format and never replace the label. Where a form checks your answers before sending, the problem is described in words beside the field rather than signalled by colour." gaps: heading: "Known gaps" intro: "A statement that claims no problems is not worth reading. These are the known shortfalls as of the date above." items: - term: "Listing photographs" description: "Property images come from the Toronto Regional Real Estate Board feed, which does not supply descriptive text for them. They are currently announced by street address rather than by what the photograph shows. A meaningful description of every interior photograph is not something the feed makes possible." - term: "Documents" description: "Some documents shared during a transaction come from third parties as untagged PDFs, which screen readers handle poorly. Ask and I will supply the same content in an accessible format." formats: heading: "Other formats" paragraphs: - "Anything on this site can be provided another way: large print, plain text, a document read aloud over the phone, or a conversation instead of a form. There is no charge and no need to explain why." - "If a listing, a market analysis or a piece of paperwork is hard to read, ask and I will send it in a format that works for you." feedback: heading: "Feedback" intro: "If something here does not work for you, I want to know. Tell me what you were trying to do and what happened, and I will reply." acknowledgement: "I will acknowledge your message and tell you what I am doing about it. If a fix will take time, I will tell you how long rather than leaving it open." escalation: "If my response does not resolve it, the brokerage can be contacted directly: Keller Williams Energy Real Estate, Brokerage, at the address above." note: "This statement is reviewed whenever the site changes in a way that affects it, and at least once a year." --- {% extends "layouts/base.html" %} {% block head %} {% endblock %} {% block body %}
