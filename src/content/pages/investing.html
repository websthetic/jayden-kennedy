Investing in Greater Toronto real estate

Four things build wealth in property. Most people can only name one, and it’s the one they control least.

  1. 01

    Amortisation

    Forced savings. In year one, roughly half of each payment stays yours as principal, and that share grows every year you hold. The quietest of the four and the most reliable.

  2. 02

    Leverage

    You buy the whole asset with a fraction of the capital. At twenty percent down, a five percent move on the property is twenty five percent on your money. It works the same in reverse, which is why the carrying cost matters more than the price.

  3. 03

    Income

    Rent collected, or rent avoided. Taxed differently, and both worth reviewing with your accountant before you structure anything.

  4. 04

    Appreciation

    Reliable over a long enough hold here. Over three years it’s a coin toss, and a coin toss isn’t a plan.

What I run on every deal

Before you commit, not in your first year of owning it.

  1. 01

    Rent evidence from the immediate area, not the city average.

  2. 02

    A vacancy assumption stated openly instead of quietly set to zero.

  3. 03

    Debt service coverage at today’s rate, and at two points higher.

  4. 04

    Full carrying cost, including the parts that get left off. Maintenance, turnover, and the month it sits empty.

What I won’t do

Build you a projection that only works if everything goes right.

If a deal needs appreciation to make sense, it isn’t a deal. It’s a bet, and you should know you’re placing one. I’d rather tell you that now than explain it in three years.
