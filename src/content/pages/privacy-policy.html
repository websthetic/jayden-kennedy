Who I am

This website is published by Jayden Kennedy, Broker, of Keller Williams Energy Real Estate, Brokerage, Independently Owned & Operated, serving Durham Region and the Greater Toronto Area.

For questions about privacy on this site, I am the person to contact. Where the brokerage holds records under its own obligations as a registrant under Ontario’s Trust in Real Estate Services Act, the brokerage’s own privacy practices also apply to those records.

What this site collects

I collect only what you type into a form, plus a small amount of technical information your browser sends automatically.

From the home valuation form
Property address, your name, email address, phone number, and anything you write in the notes field.
From the contact and enquiry forms
Your name, email address, phone number where you provide it, and the content of your message.
From the subscribe form
Your email address.
Automatically
Your browser sends an IP address, browser type, and the page you arrived from with every request. This is ordinary web traffic information and I do not use it to identify you.

I do not ask for a Social Insurance Number, date of birth, banking details, or identity documents through this website. If a transaction reaches the stage where those are required, they are collected through the brokerage under separate paperwork, never through this site.

Why I collect it

To answer you. A valuation request is answered with a valuation. An enquiry is answered with a reply. A subscription is answered with the emails you subscribed to.

I do not sell your information, rent it, or trade it to lead-generation services.

Listing data

Listing information on this site is provided by the Toronto Regional Real Estate Board and is deemed reliable but not guaranteed accurate. It is displayed under the terms of my board membership. Nothing you enter on this site is sent back to the board.

{# CONFIRM: browser-only storage, or against an account #}

Saved searches and saved listings, if you use them, are stored in your browser only. Clearing your browser data removes them and I never see them.

Reviews

Reviews shown on this site are published from my Google Business Profile through Google’s API and are displayed as Google returns them. I do not retype, edit, or reorder them. Reviewer names are held back on this website; the originals are public and verifiable on Google. Google’s own privacy terms govern the review you leave there.

Email and text messages

I email or call you because you asked me to. If you send an enquiry, my reply is a response to your request, not marketing.

Subscribing is an express consent under Canada’s anti-spam legislation. Every email carries an unsubscribe link that works within ten business days, and the sender address and mailing address appear in every message. Unsubscribing from the list does not affect a conversation already underway about a property.

Where your information is stored

Some of the services used to run this site store data on servers outside Canada, including in the United States. Where that is the case, your information may be accessible to courts and law enforcement in that country under its laws. If you would rather not have a form submission handled this way, phone or email me directly instead.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not lead anywhere are kept for [CONFIRM · defined period] and then deleted.

Records connected to a completed or attempted trade in real estate are retained for the period required of a registrant under Ontario’s real estate legislation, which is longer, and I cannot delete those on request during that period.

Mailing list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe.

Your choices

You can ask me what information I hold about you, ask for a copy of it, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it where I am not required to keep it. You can withdraw consent to marketing at any time. Ask by email and I will respond within thirty days.

If you are not satisfied with how I have handled a privacy question, you can raise it with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
