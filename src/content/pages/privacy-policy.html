--- title: Privacy Policy | Jayden Kennedy Real Estate description: How soldbykennedy.ca collects, uses, discloses, and stores your personal information, and the choices you have about it. hero: heading: Privacy Policy standfirst: How this website collects, uses, discloses, and stores your personal information and what you can ask me to do with it. lastUpdated: 2026-01-01 whoIAm: heading: 1 · Who I am paragraphs: - This website is published by Jayden Kennedy, Broker, of Keller Williams Energy Real Estate, Brokerage, Independently Owned & Operated, serving Durham Region and the Greater Toronto Area. In this policy, “I” and “me” mean Jayden Kennedy; “this site” means soldbykennedy.ca. - “Personal information” means information about an identifiable individual, as that term is used in Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act. That is the law this policy is written under. scope: heading: 2 · What this policy covers paragraphs: - This policy covers information collected through this website and through the emails, calls, and messages that follow from it. It is my policy, for my practice. - Two other sets of practices sit alongside it. The brokerage holds records under its own obligations as a registrant under Ontario's Trust in Real Estate Services Act, and its practices govern those records. Keller Williams Realty, LLC, the franchisor, publishes a separate corporate privacy policy for the systems it operates; that policy states plainly that it does not cover the practices of individual market centres or their agents. Where your information sits with one of them rather than with me, theirs applies and not this one. whatICollect: heading: 3 · What I collect intro: Only what you type into a form, plus a small amount of technical information your browser sends automatically. items: - term: From the home valuation form description: Property address, your name, email address, phone number, your timeframe, and anything you write in the notes field. - term: From the contact and enquiry forms description: Your name, email address, phone number where you provide it, and the content of your message. - term: From the subscribe form description: Your email address. - term: Automatically description: Your browser sends an IP address, browser and device type, and the page you arrived from with every request. This is ordinary web traffic information and I do not use it to identify you. paragraphs: - I do not ask for a Social Insurance Number, date of birth, banking details, or identity documents through this website. If a transaction reaches the stage where those are required, they are collected through the brokerage under separate paperwork, never through this site. - Please do not send sensitive information by email. Email is not a secure channel, and nothing on this site needs a SIN, an account number, or a scan of your identification to be useful to you. why: heading: 4 · Why I collect it paragraphs: - To answer you. A valuation request is answered with a valuation. An enquiry is answered with a reply. A subscription is answered with the emails you subscribed to. Where a conversation becomes a transaction, the same information is used to carry out the work you have engaged me for, and to meet the record-keeping obligations that come with it. - I do not sell your personal information, rent it, or trade it to lead-generation services. It is not shared with advertisers, and there is no arrangement under which anyone pays for access to it. - Consent is how collection is justified here. Filling in a form is consent to be answered through it. You can withdraw consent at any time, subject to the records I am required to keep, which are described in section 12. listingData: heading: 5 · Listing data paragraphs: - Listing information on this site is provided by the Toronto Regional Real Estate Board and is deemed reliable but not guaranteed accurate. It is displayed under the terms of my board membership. Nothing you enter on this site is sent back to the board. - Saved searches and saved listings, if you use them, are stored in your browser only. Clearing your browser data removes them and I never see them. reviews: heading: 6 · Reviews paragraphs: - The reviews on this site are quoted from my Google Business Profile. Five are shown and I chose which five. The wording is reproduced as it was written — nothing is rewritten, corrected, or shortened, and where a review runs long the remainder sits behind a “Read more” control rather than being trimmed. Each one carries the reviewer's name and the month it was posted, exactly as it appears on Google. - The reviews page links to the full, unfiltered list at the source, so you can read everything on the profile including anything I have not quoted here. A review you leave on Google is public there by your own choice, and is governed by Google's terms rather than by this policy. - If you have written one of these and would rather it not appear on this site, email me and I will take it off the page. It stays on Google either way — that is yours to remove, not mine. cookies: heading: 7 · Cookies and analytics paragraphs: - This site sets no advertising cookies and runs no third-party analytics. Nothing on this site follows you to another website. No profile is built about you from your browsing, and no automated system makes decisions about you. email: heading: 8 · Email, calls, and texts paragraphs: - I email, call, or text you because you asked me to. If you send an enquiry, my reply is a response to your request, not marketing. - Subscribing is an express consent under Canada's anti-spam legislation. Every email carries an unsubscribe link that works within ten business days, and the sender address and mailing address appear in every message. Unsubscribing from the list does not affect a conversation already underway about a property. - Giving me your phone number is consent to be called or texted about what you asked about. That consent is separate from the mailing list and can be withdrawn on its own — say so in a reply, or tell me on the phone, and it stops. I do not make unsolicited marketing calls to numbers I have not been given directly. sharing: heading: 9 · Who else sees it paragraphs: - Form submissions reach me through [CONFIRM · email, CRM, or form service]. That provider processes the submission on my behalf, under contract, and is not permitted to use it for its own purposes. - "Beyond that, your information is disclosed only in these circumstances:" items: - term: To the brokerage description: Where a transaction requires it, or where the brokerage's obligations as a registrant require it. - term: To the people a deal needs description: Lawyers, lenders, inspectors, and other parties you and I agree to involve. - term: Where the law requires it description: To a court, a regulator, or a law enforcement agency acting under authority. I do not volunteer your information to anyone on request without that authority. storage: heading: 10 · Where it is stored paragraphs: - Some of the services used to run this site store data on servers outside Canada, including in the United States. Where that is the case, your information is subject to the laws of that country, and courts or law enforcement there may be entitled to access it. If you would rather not have a form submission handled that way, phone or email me directly instead and it will not pass through those systems. security: heading: 11 · Security paragraphs: - This site is served over an encrypted connection, and the services that handle submissions are chosen for their own security practices. Access to enquiries is limited to me and, where a transaction requires it, the brokerage. - No system is completely secure, and I would rather say that than imply otherwise. If you have reason to think a message you sent me has been intercepted, or you have found a problem with this site, tell me using the details in section 17 and I will look into it. retention: heading: 12 · How long I keep it paragraphs: - Enquiries that do not lead anywhere are kept for [CONFIRM · defined period] and then deleted. - Records connected to a completed or attempted trade in real estate are retained for the period required of a registrant under Ontario's real estate legislation, which is longer. I cannot delete those on request during that period, and I will tell you so rather than agreeing and quietly not doing it. - Mailing list addresses are kept until you unsubscribe. yourChoices: heading: 13 · Your rights and choices items: - term: Access description: Ask what information I hold about you and ask for a copy of it. - term: Correction description: Ask me to correct anything that is wrong or out of date. - term: Deletion description: Ask me to delete it, where I am not required to keep it under section 12. - term: Withdrawing consent description: Stop the mailing list, the calls, the texts, or all three, at any time and without explaining why. paragraphs: - Ask by email and I will respond within thirty days. There is no charge. If I cannot do what you have asked, I will tell you which of the reasons above applies. - If you are not satisfied with how I have handled a privacy question, raise it with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, which oversees compliance with the legislation this policy is written under. minors: heading: 14 · Young people paragraphs: - This site is meant for adults buying, selling, or holding property. It is not directed at children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under sixteen. If you believe a young person has sent me information through this site, tell me and I will delete it. thirdParties: heading: 15 · Other websites paragraphs: - This site links out — to Google, to the board's listing data, to Instagram, and occasionally to a news article or a municipal page. Those sites have their own privacy practices and this policy does not cover them. A link is not an endorsement of how the other site handles your information. changes: heading: 16 · Changes to this policy paragraphs: - The date at the top is the date this policy last changed. If the way information is handled here changes in a way that matters — a new analytics tool, a different form provider, a mailing list where there was none — this page changes with it, and the date moves. It is not backdated and old versions are not quietly overwritten in substance without the date reflecting it. contact: heading: 17 · Contact paragraphs: - Jayden Kennedy, Broker — the person responsible for personal information collected through this site. note: Because email is not always secure, please do not include a Social Insurance Number, an account number, or identification documents in a message to me. permalink: /privacy-policy/ --- {% extends "layouts/base.html" %} {% block head %} {% endblock %} {% block body %} {# STRUCTURE NOTE Rebuilt to the shape of the Keller Williams corporate policy at legal.kw.com/privacy-policy: numbered sections, terms defined before they are used, and the sections that policy carries which this one did not — security, minors, third-party links, changes, an explicit no-sale statement, and a warning against emailing sensitive data.
